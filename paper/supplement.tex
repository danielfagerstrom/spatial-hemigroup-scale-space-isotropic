This supplement belongs to the paper of the same title. The paper prints the statements, the
proofs of the classification and of Proposition~\ref{prop:profile-one-dimension-down}, and the
proofs of the statements that are proved in print only.
Six lemmas that Lean checked are stated in \S\ref{supp:proofs} and not in the paper, which
cites them by their numbers here. The supplement has what the formal verification covers: the account of the trust base
(\S\ref{supp:trust}), the seven cited facts as the formal development assumes them
(\S\ref{supp:cited-facts}), the proofs of the statements that Lean checked
(\S\ref{supp:proofs}), and the list of the declarations of the formal development with Lean's
report (\S\ref{supp:verification}). The statements, sections, equations and tables of the
supplement are numbered S.1, S.2, and so on. A number without an S, as in Theorem~3.6, \S3.3 or
Appendix~A, is a number of the paper, and the notation is that of the paper
(Table~\ref{tab:notation}).
